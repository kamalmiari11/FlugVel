What it does

Six screens. One knob.

Turn to move, click to switch screens. Reorder or hide any of them.

Plane tracker

Nearest aircraft overhead — callsign, altitude, speed, bearing. Quiet sky gets a random quote instead.

Weather

3–7 day forecast. Tap in for the hourly breakdown.

Calendar

Up to four iCal feeds, merged into one agenda. No duplicates, I promise.

Focus timer

Pomodoro, but on a microcontroller that also watches planes.

Games

Flappy Plane and Paddle Catch work. Two more are "coming soon" (narrator: they are not soon).

Settings

Theme, updates, factory reset — the usual suspects.

No app, no account

It runs its own settings page.

Open it, poke around, close it. That's the whole flow.
