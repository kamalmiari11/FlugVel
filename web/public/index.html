A little box that watches the sky so I don't have to.

Flights, weather, your calendar, your notes, stock prices, a focus timer, two working games. All on one knob. No app. You're looking at it right now, actually — this whole page is running on its screen.

Read the manual

Eight screens. One knob.

Turn to move, click to switch screens. Reorder or hide any of them.

Plane tracker

Nearest aircraft overhead — callsign, altitude, speed, bearing. Quiet sky gets a random quote instead.

Weather

3–7 day forecast. Tap in for the hourly breakdown.

Calendar

Up to four iCal feeds, merged into one agenda. No duplicates, I promise.

Notes

Pulled straight from a Notion page — group it by day with the first one opened automatically, and tick things off right on the device.

Stocks

Price and day change for up to ten tickers you pick — bring your own free API key, no subscription needed.

Focus timer

Pomodoro, but on a microcontroller that also watches planes.

Games

Flappy Plane and Paddle Catch work. Two more are "coming soon" (narrator: they are not soon).

Settings

Theme, updates, factory reset — the usual suspects.
