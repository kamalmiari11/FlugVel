One little box for the sky above, the day ahead, and everything in between.

Flights. Weather. Your day. The markets. Your notes. A timer. A couple of games for when you need a break.All in one little box. All at the turn of a knob.No app. No phone. No endless tapping.In fact, you’re already using it. This whole page is running on its screen.

See how it works

What's on it

Eight screens. You choose which ones show up and in what order.

Plane tracker

The closest plane above you: callsign, altitude, speed and direction. No planes? You get a quote.

Weather

Up to a week ahead. Click a day for the hourly view.

Calendar

Link your Google, iCloud or Outlook calendar. Up to four, merged into one list.

Notes

Your to-do list from Notion, on your desk. Check things off with the knob.

Stocks

Up to ten tickers. Green or red, at a glance.

Focus timer

Set a focus block and a break. It can lock the screen until you're done.

Games

Four games on one knob. Harder than it sounds.

Settings

Pick a theme, check for updates, or start over.

Set it up from your phone

Scan the code on the screen and a setup page opens. No app to install.
