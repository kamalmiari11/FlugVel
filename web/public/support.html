Support

Stuck on something? Check the questions below first — most of what comes up is covered there. Still stuck, or found something that looks like a bug? The form at the bottom goes straight to a real inbox, not a ticket queue.

Common questions

Before you write in

My device won't connect to Wi-Fi

Double-check the password and that you picked the right network during setup — the setup page stays open for another attempt if it fails. If you're not even seeing the setup screen on first boot, try a power cycle; if it still doesn't appear, a factory reset (hold both buttons for 15 seconds) usually clears it. Full walkthrough: Manual §1.

A screen says "unavailable" or "not found"

This is normal, not a bug report waiting to happen. "Not found" means whatever you configured (a stock ticker, usually) doesn't actually exist — check the spelling in the setup console. "Unavailable" is a transient hiccup — a network blip, a rate limit — that a later refresh usually clears on its own. See Manual §8 for the specific cases.

How do I install a firmware update?

FlugVel checks for updates automatically after connecting to Wi-Fi, and any time you pick Update from the Settings menu. If one's available, it shows the version and release notes and asks before downloading — nothing installs without you confirming. Details: Manual §6.

How do I reset my device?

Hold both buttons (the knob's click button and the other one) together for 15 seconds. This clears saved Wi-Fi, location, theme and every other preference — you'll go through first-time setup again afterward. Same thing is also available from Settings → Factory Reset. See Manual §7.

Is my data private?

Short version: your device talks directly to the services you configure it for (weather, flights, your own calendar/notes/stock accounts) — there's no FlugVel-run server collecting any of that. Full details are in the privacy policy.

I found a bug, or want to suggest something

That's exactly what the form below is for — include your firmware version (Settings → Device Info) and what you were doing when it happened, and it's much easier to track down.
