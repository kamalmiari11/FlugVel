Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. FlugVel is a personal project, not a company — this page is written in plain language rather than legal boilerplate, and covers exactly what this site does.

What this site collects

If you use the "Get updates" signup form, it stores the email address you enter, along with the date you signed up. That's the only personal information this site asks visitors for.

Simply reading the site — the overview page or the manual — doesn't collect or store anything about you. There's no tracking script, no third-party ad network, and no analytics running on this site as of this writing. If that changes, this page will be updated to say so, and any analytics added will be the cookieless, non-tracking kind rather than something that profiles visitors.

Cookies

The only cookie this site sets is on the private admin dashboard, used to keep the site owner logged in. It's what's called a "strictly necessary" cookie — it doesn't track you, isn't used for advertising, and isn't shared with anyone. It's not set at all unless someone actually logs in, which nobody but the owner can do. Because of that, this site doesn't show a cookie consent banner; there's nothing here that legally requires one.

What the email address is used for

Signing up gets you a one-time welcome email, and occasional emails when there's an actual firmware or feature update — nothing else. Your address is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond that. Every email includes a way to be removed (currently: reply and ask).

Who this data passes through

Two outside services are involved in running this site, and both only see what's strictly needed to do their job:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database (Cloudflare Pages and D1). Your email address, if you sign up, is stored in that database.
  • Resend is the email service used to actually send the welcome/update emails. It sees the email address you enter and the content of the email being sent — nothing more.

Neither service is authorized to use this data for anything beyond delivering this site and its emails.

The physical device

FlugVel devices are self-hosted — a unit checks in with this website's server periodically to report its status and check for firmware updates, using a shared device key rather than any personal login. Device check-ins may include a device ID, approximate signal strength, and firmware version, but nothing about what you personally do with your unit (your flights, weather, calendar, or notes stay on the device and the third-party services you configured it to use directly — they don't pass through or get stored on this website).

Removing your data

To be removed from the update list, reply to any email from it and ask, or reach out through wherever you found this site. Since there's no account system for visitors, there's no login to delete — asking is genuinely the whole process.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects or how it's used ever changes, this page will be updated to reflect that, with the "last updated" date above changed accordingly.
