Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. FlugVel is a personal project, not a company. This page explains in plain language what this site does with your data.

What this site collects

The email signup form stores your email address and the date you signed up. The support form sends your name, email and message to my inbox and doesn't store them on the site. Nothing else is collected.

Reading the site collects nothing. There's no tracking, no ads and no analytics. If that ever changes, this page will say so, and any analytics would be the cookieless kind that doesn't profile you.

Cookies

The only cookie is on the private admin dashboard, to keep me logged in. It's a strictly necessary cookie: it doesn't track you and isn't shared. Visitors never get it, which is why there's no cookie banner.

What the email address is used for

You get one welcome email, then an email when there's a firmware or feature update. Your address is never sold or shared, and every email has an unsubscribe link that deletes it.

Who this data passes through

Two outside services run this site, and each only sees what it needs:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database (Cloudflare Pages and D1). Your email address, if you sign up, is stored in that database.
  • Resend is the email service used to actually send the welcome/update emails. It sees your email address and the content of the email being sent.

Neither may use this data for anything except running the site and sending its emails.

The physical device

A FlugVel device checks in with this site now and then to report its status and look for firmware updates. A check-in includes a device ID, signal strength and firmware version. It never includes what you use the device for: your flights, weather, calendar and notes go straight between the device and the services you set up, not through this site.

Removing your data

Use the unsubscribe link in any update email, or your mail app's own unsubscribe button. Either one deletes your address completely; it isn't kept on a list. If a link doesn't work, reply to any email and I'll remove you.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
